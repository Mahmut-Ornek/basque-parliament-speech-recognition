# Error-Type & Morphology Analysis

Generated by `src/error_types.py` (full machine-readable table in `results/error_types.csv`, covering all 8 baselines and 12 fine-tuned runs across `dev` and `test`). References and hypotheses are normalised with `src/normalize.py` and aligned at the word level using `jiwer`.

## Rules and `dev` Calibration

Because nobody in the team speaks Basque, every word error is assigned by a deterministic rule (checked in priority order):

1. **Number error:** reference or predicted word contains a digit (`0–9`). The corpus writes numbers as Basque words, so outputting digits counts as a number-formatting error.
2. **Suffix-only error:** a substituted word pair sharing a prefix of **at least 4 characters** and **at least 60% of the longer word** (correct stem, wrong inflectional/agglutinative ending).
3. **Other substitution / Deletion / Insertion:** all remaining alignment errors.
4. **Looping clips:** clips with sustained consecutive n-gram repetitions or severe repetition near the `max_new_tokens=128` decoding ceiling.

**Weighted WER** assigns weight **0.5** to suffix-only errors and **1.0** to all other errors. The 60% prefix threshold and 0.5 weight were fixed on `dev` before inspecting `test`. Sensitivity on `dev` (`min_prefix=4`):

| Prefix Ratio Threshold | `hitz-ctc` | `parakeet-v3-basque` | `whisper-small-zeroshot` | `ft-ours_seed3` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `>= 0.50` | 228 (24.1%) | 197 (24.7%) | 1,072 (6.1%) | 721 (18.6%) |
| **`>= 0.60` (selected)** | **168 (17.8%)** | **147 (18.4%)** | **790 (4.5%)** | **558 (14.4%)** |
| `>= 0.70` | 136 (14.4%) | 123 (15.4%) | 497 (2.8%) | 388 (10.0%) |

`0.60` sits cleanly in the middle—strict enough to avoid counting unrelated short-prefix pairs as morphological matches while retaining genuine Basque case/verb-ending mismatches.
